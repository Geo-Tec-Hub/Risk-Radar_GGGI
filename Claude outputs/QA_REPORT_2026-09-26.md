## How the expert and community score works

- For each variable, the person's figure is scaled against the **province's official range** for that variable. If the figure falls outside that range, the range is widened to include it.
- The hazard and exposure indexes use the **same weights** as the official score.
- H × E is then rescaled with the **official product bounds** for the same profile and period.
- So an expert score sits on the same scale as the published score for that division. **Test: an expert who enters exactly the official figures gets exactly the official score back.** An automated test checks this.
- If a province has no official data yet, there is no range to scale against. The form then shows the raw H × E and says so.
