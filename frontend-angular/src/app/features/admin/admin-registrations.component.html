Every self-registration sits here until approved or rejected. Approving grants a role and, for a data officer or expert, binds them to exactly one province (SRS §3.2) and to the sectors they may write to — nothing they submit is written until that happens. The areas an applicant asked for are a request, not a grant: the boxes start empty, and an approval grants only what you tick.
